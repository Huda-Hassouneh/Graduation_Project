# Literature review

**Status: not yet conducted.** No sources have been reviewed or cited, and no novelty claim is
made anywhere in this repository. The review is scheduled before Milestone 5 (diagnosis
method selection). Its scope:

1. Multirotor fault detection and isolation (actuator effectiveness loss, mass/payload change),
   both residual/observer-based and data-driven.
2. Change detection: CUSUM, GLR, innovation (χ²) tests; recursive least squares with forgetting.
3. Active fault diagnosis and input design for identifiability / information gain.
4. Fault-tolerant control and safe recovery for multirotors, including reachability,
   control barrier functions and MPC with constraints.
5. Energy-aware planning and diagnosis for UAVs.
6. Facade and near-structure UAV operations, and wall-proximity aerodynamics.

Rules: verifiable sources only (DOI or stable URL); record access dates; never cite a work
that has not been located and read.
